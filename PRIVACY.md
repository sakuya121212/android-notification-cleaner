# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

NotificationCleaner processes notification titles, text, source application names, and timestamps only on the device in order to show the notification history requested by the user. This information is stored in the app database and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

The user can delete the stored notification history from within the app. Disabling an app in the settings also removes its stored history.

Before publishing, replace this document with a policy that identifies the publisher, provides a contact method, and accurately reflects the released app and any third-party services.
